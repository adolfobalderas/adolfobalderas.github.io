About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Southern California, working with Professor Sung Kyu Lim in the Southern California Computer-Aided Design Lab.

My interests span VLSI design, physical design automation, advanced semiconductor packaging, AI hardware, interconnects, and energy-efficient computing. I am particularly interested in connecting architecture, circuit design, physical implementation, and packaging constraints.
